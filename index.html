Who We Are & Why We Exist

Every Child Deserves the Power of Musical Expression.

At Music For All Hearts, we envision a future where young people can pursue their passion for music without the weight of financial burdens holding them back. We believe every child—regardless of socioeconomic background or health-related challenges—deserves the opportunity to learn, express, and thrive.

By providing free music lessons, we bridge the gap for families facing hardship. Our dedicated instructors are accomplished performers and philanthropic mentors who foster resilience, empathy, and lifelong confidence.

Zero Financial Barriers

100% tuition-free lesson packages redeemed throughout the calendar year for qualifying families.

Virtual & In-Person

Flexible 1-on-1 lessons via Zoom/Skype, case-by-case in-person studio sessions, and hospital group classes.

What We Offer

Comprehensive, Heart-Centered Music Programs

12-Week Core Course

Vocal Lessons

Designed to empower students with a comprehensive foundation in vocal technique while unlocking confidence and stage presence in a friendly, supportive environment.

  • Up to 60-minute personalized sessions
  • Breath support, pitch control & articulation
  • Performance etiquette & stage confidence
  • Tailored 1-on-1 feedback for every singer

Beginner & Intermediate

Instrumental (Piano & Guitar)

Free instrumental lessons for beginners and intermediates! No prior experience is required—just excitement to learn and a 61+ key keyboard, piano, or guitar.

  • Scales, chords, sight-reading & music theory
  • Weekly sessions for steady growth
  • No prior musical experience needed
  • Option to study multiple instruments
